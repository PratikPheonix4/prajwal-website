Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and the control you have over it.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Who we are

PRAJWAL ("we", "us") is building a payment app that lets international visitors to India pay merchants through UPI QR codes using their international cards. We are based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. This policy covers this website and any early-access or waitlist sign-ups.

What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address and message when you use the contact form or join a waitlist.
  • Usage data: basic technical data such as browser type, device, pages visited and approximate location, collected through cookies or similar tools.
  • Payment app data: when the app launches, verification and transaction details will be needed to process payments. We will show a separate notice in the app before collecting any of it.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiries and send updates you asked for.
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and security obligations.

We do not sell your personal data.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help us run the website and communicate with you (hosting, email and analytics), and with authorities when the law requires it. Providers may only use data to perform services for us. Some may store data outside India.

Cookies

We use cookies to keep the site working and to measure traffic. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; parts of the site may not work as expected if you do.

Retention and security

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires, then delete or anonymise it. We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no online system is completely secure.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance. Write to us at prateekgwip988@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our services are not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you on the site or by email.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use our contact page.
